# 0002. Object dimensions and height model

## Context

Footprints lived in `Structure.GRID_SIZES` (buildings only), draw sizes were hard-coded
per kind in `static/renderer.js`, and nothing recorded how tall an object is. Floors,
bridges and line of sight need a height. `docs/assets.md`, generated by
`scripts/asset_inventory.py`, measures every sprite.

## Decision

- `game/dimensions.py` holds one `ObjectDimensions` per `ObjectKind`: footprint (whole
  tiles, width along columns, depth along rows), height, and the `SpriteBox` the sprite
  is drawn in. Every object kind has an entry; one tile by convention for trees, rocks,
  wheat, NPCs and fauna.
- **Height unit: tiles, in steps of half a tile** (`HEIGHT_STEP`). One tile of height is
  one storey: 64 px of vertical extent in the 3/4 view. A building's height is its
  sprite frame height minus its footprint depth (house 1, tower 2, monastery 2, castle,
  barracks and archery 1). A sprite-sheet object's height is the opaque height of its
  first frame, as drawn, above its footprint, rounded up to the half tile: tree 1,
  NPCs and fauna 0.5, rocks and wheat 0.
- **Walkable levels** (the floors ticket implements them): every tile gets an
  `elevation` in the same unit, 0 for the ground. A structure exposes a walkable level
  only by declaring it (a tower's top at its height); its other tiles stay blocked.
  An NPC or creature stands at the elevation of the tile or level it is on.
- **Pathfinding** reads elevation as an edge rule: a step between two adjacent tiles is
  allowed when their elevations are equal, or differ by one step through a tile marked
  as a ramp or stairs. Costs stay per tile.
- **Line of sight** between two points compares, along the tiles the segment crosses,
  the interpolated height of the segment (shooter's elevation plus its height, target's
  elevation plus its height) with each crossed tile's elevation plus the height of the
  structure or resource on it. A taller blocker cuts the line.
- The state sends `footprint`, `height` and `sprite` (size and ground anchor, in tiles)
  for every resource, NPC and creature; the renderer multiplies by `tileSize` and no
  longer decides any per-kind size (`docs/api.md`).

## Considered options

- Height in storeys (integers): too coarse for NPCs and trees, which stand below a
  storey but must still block or be seen.
- Height in pixels: ties the model to the 64 px tiles and the sprite pack.
- Sprite box kept in the client: two places would decide the draw size, against rule 1
  of `.claude/rules/architecture.md`.

## Consequences

The sprite boxes reproduce the previous draw sizes exactly (`tests/test_dimensions.py`
compares them with the old hard-coded values). Every building is still drawn with the
house sprite, stretched to its footprint width and 1.5 times as tall: only houses and
the storage hut are built today. Pathfinding and line of sight do not read heights yet.
