HOW_TO_PLAY

// MISSION BRIEFING

You are the commander of a small colony. Your units are autonomous NPCs — they idle, wander, and survive on their own.

Your role is to give them orders — gather resources, build structures, farm wheat, and keep everyone fed.

Use your voice, the text terminal, or manual clicks to command your units.

// VOICE COMMANDS

G HOLD TO TALK

Hold G and speak naturally. The AI will interpret your intent and dispatch orders to the right NPCs.

EXAMPLES:

"Send Marcus to chop wood"

"Have everyone gather stone"

"Tell Luna to build a house"

"Plant wheat near the river"

// TEXT TERMINAL

Prefer typing? Use the command input at the bottom of the screen.

Type the same natural-language orders you would speak — the AI interprets them identically.

> tell Felix to mine rock
> everyone eat

// MANUAL CONTROLS

↑ MOVE CAMERA
←↓→ ARROW KEYS TO PAN

Click on an NPC to select it, then use the action buttons on the left panel to issue manual orders:

[CHOP_WOOD] · [MINE_ROCK] · [BUILD] · [PLANT] · [COLLECT] · [EAT]

// SURVIVAL TIPS

🌾 NPCs get hungry over time — plant & collect wheat, then order them to [EAT] before they starve.

🪵 Wood and stone are needed to [BUILD] structures.

📋 Check the ROSTER panel to monitor all your units at once.

📜 The EVENT_LOG shows every action and AI interpretation in real time.

Good luck, Commander.
